Verdict

The current palette is functional, readable, and entirely generic - the default indigo-on-navy you've seen on a thousand SaaS dashboards since 2022.

Nothing about it whispers Traveller: no 1977 wargame heritage, no ship's-computer texture, no found-document feel. It works; it does not belong.

§ 01  ·  Findings

Five specific problems.

The accent pair is the most over-used in modern web design.

#b8c0ff + #5c7cfa is essentially Tailwind indigo-300 on indigo-500. It's the visual default of every AI-generated dark dashboard. The campaign deserves a signature, not a fallback.

The three background tones are too compressed.

#1a1a2e, #16213e, #0f0f23 sit within ~6 luminance points of each other. Sidebar, body, and cards all blur together; surface hierarchy collapses. Either widen the steps or lean into a single-surface aesthetic.

Every chrome color lives in the same hue band.

Backgrounds, borders, headings, links, faint text - all sit between 230° and 270° hue. The page is monochrome until a status tag forces in a green or red, which then reads as foreign. There's no harmonic counterweight.

Headings, links, and brand all share one accent.

--accent is used for h1, h2, the brand mark, every link, code highlights, and active nav. Color carries no information - size is the only hierarchy signal.

Status colors clash with the muted base.

The Bootstrap-bright traffic-light set (#69db7c, #ffd43b, #ff6b6b) is louder than anything else on the page. They feel pasted in, not part of the same world.

Zero atmosphere for a science-fiction subject.

Solid navy + rectangular cards. No CRT phosphor, no star-chart bone-white, no faded-paper dossier. The biggest wasted opportunity isn't any single color - it's that the palette commits to nothing thematic at all.

§ 03  ·  Recommendation

Pick Phosphor.

It does the one thing the current palette doesn't: it commits to a place. A campaign viewer for a 1977-rooted sci-fi RPG should look like a ship's reference terminal, not a Notion clone.

Amber-on-coal solves all five findings at once - it's specific, the surface steps widen, the hue pivots out of indigo monoculture, and the warn/good greens stop reading as imports because the whole base is warm.

Star-Chart is the safe modern choice if Phosphor feels too costumed. Dossier is the bold inversion play.
